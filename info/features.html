Full Feature List

A complete frontend workflow for school operations, ready to connect to a Supabase backend.

Admin Portal

The command center for school administrators — branch admins, principals, and school directors.

  • Student Enrollment & Profiles — Full student records: personal info, emergency contacts, health data, enrollment history, and financial records. Sensitive identifiers should be stored only in protected backend fields when a school has a legitimate need for them.
  • Teacher Management — Add, edit, and assign teachers to classes. View their schedule, assigned classes, and student roster.
  • Class & Scheduling — Create classes by grade and subject, assign teachers, build rosters. Schedule stored per-class.
  • Attendance Management — View and override attendance by class and date. Class-level summary stats.
  • Payments & Billing — Track tuition, fees, and activity charges. Record payments, view balances, mark charges as paid/partial/unpaid.
  • Families — View all parents and guardians. See linked students, outstanding balances, and contact information.
  • Lunch Menu — Manage the weekly cafeteria menu. Mark items as halal-certified.
  • Forms — Create required and optional forms for families (permission slips, surveys, registration).
  • Events — Post school events to the calendar. Enable registration for events that require it.
  • Communications — Send mass messages to all parents, all teachers, or a specific grade level.
  • Documents — Upload and manage shared documents (handbooks, calendars, policies).
  • Report Cards — View and generate student report cards based on class averages.
  • Announcements — Post school-wide or grade-specific announcements with priority flagging.
  • Admissions Pipeline — Track applicants from New and Reviewing through Accepted, Waitlisted, Denied, and Enrolled. Accepted applicants can be converted directly into student records.
  • School Year Closeout & Rollover — Archive a completed year, start the next one, and review every student before rollover. Promote, repeat a grade, graduate, mark not returning, or skip undecided students. Optionally copy class structures into the new year with clean rosters.
  • CSV Roster Tools — Import student rosters in bulk and export current student data for review or migration.
  • Audit Logs — Full chronological log of every administrative action with user and timestamp.
  • Settings — Configure branch info, notifications, and account preferences.

Teacher Portal

Purpose-built for teachers — focused on their classes and their students.

  • Dashboard — Today's classes, attendance status, upcoming events, and recent announcements.
  • My Classes — View all assigned classes with roster size, room, and schedule. Quick links to gradebook and attendance.
  • My Students — All students across all assigned classes in one searchable list.
  • Attendance — Mark Present, Absent, Tardy, or Excused per student per class per day. Cumulative stats shown.
  • Gradebook — Inline grade entry per assignment per student. Grades auto-save with 600ms debounce. Color-coded by letter grade.
  • Assignments — Create and manage assignments by type (Homework, Quiz, Exam, Project). Set due dates and point values.
  • Report Cards — View class averages and letter grades for every student in your classes.
  • Schedule — Weekly view of all assigned classes by day.
  • Messages — Read and respond to messages from parents and administrators.

Parent Portal

Full visibility into their child's school life — designed to keep families informed and engaged.

  • Home Dashboard — At-a-glance: current grades, attendance rate, account balance, and upcoming events.
  • Grades — Per-class averages and individual assignment scores. Letter grade color coding.
  • Attendance — Full attendance history with present/absent/tardy/excused breakdown and rate calculation.
  • Classes — Child's enrolled classes, teachers, rooms, and schedules.
  • Cafeteria — Weekly lunch menu with halal certification status and pricing.
  • Forms — View and submit required and optional school forms.
  • Events — School calendar with one-click event registration.
  • Expenses — Full billing history, payment status, and outstanding balances.
  • Messages — Direct messaging with teachers and administration.
  • Photos — View school photo albums.
  • Documents — Download handbooks, calendars, and school policies.
  • Multiple Children — Parents with multiple enrolled children can switch between them instantly.

Student Portal

A focused portal where students can see their own academic information without accessing family or administrative data.

  • Dashboard — Overall average, attendance rate, enrolled classes, upcoming assignments, and school announcements.
  • Assignments — Upcoming classwork with due dates, class, type, and point values.
  • Grades & Attendance — Current class performance and personal attendance history.
  • Classes, Calendar, Documents & Messages — Student-specific access to the school information they need.

Super Admin Portal

Platform-level administration for the team running the SMS platform.

  • Create and manage school accounts
  • Manage branches per school
  • Platform-wide user management
  • Billing and subscription management
  • Platform security and audit logs
  • Theme and integration settings
