Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — we offer a 30-day free trial on all plans. No credit card required to start.

How does pricing work for multiple schools?

Enterprise pricing is per-school-network, not per-campus. Contact us for a custom quote based on your number of schools and students.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits or Islamic schools?

Yes. We offer 20% discounts for registered non-profit schools and additional consideration for Islamic schools. Contact us to apply.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, ACH bank transfer, and annual invoicing for Enterprise customers.
